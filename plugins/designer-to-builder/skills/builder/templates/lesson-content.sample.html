4Today's lesson

Screen states: loading → error → empty → results

Why this one: the biggest gap above is that “still loading” and “nothing found” look the same. Learning the four states fixes that here and in every screen you build.

Step 3 · Key words

Three words you'll see

State
What the app is remembering right now, like what you typed in the search box.
Like a sticky note the page keeps, and it redraws when the note changes.
Loading state
What the screen shows while it waits for data to arrive.
Like a “saving…” spinner. The answer isn't ready, but the screen tells you it's working.
Conditional rendering
Showing different things depending on a condition.
Like switching a component between its Empty and Filled variants.
